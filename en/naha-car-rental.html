3 ways to get your car

✈️ Free airport shuttle

Take our free shuttle from the rental-car bus stop 11-B at Naha Airport. Departs every hour on the hour (9:00-19:00). No reservation needed.

🏨 Delivery to your hotel

We bring the car to your hotel or home in Naha City or Tomigusuku City and pick it up there. ¥1,000 each way.

🚶 Come to our office

HANDYMAN Parking 2, 180-1 Nakaji, Tomigusuku, Okinawa.

※ Cars cannot be handed over inside Naha Airport (local rule). Please use the free shuttle or hotel delivery.

How to find the shuttle at Naha Airport

  1. Go to the 1st floor (arrivals) and leave the terminal through Exit 4.
  2. Cross the crosswalk and turn right.
  3. Wait at the Rental Car Shuttle Bus stop 11-B. Our shuttle leaves at :00 every hour.
  4. On the way back, the shuttle leaves our office at :30 every hour (9:30-18:30) and takes you to the airport for free.

Driver's license: please check before you book

  • Bring all three: passport, your home-country license, and an International Driving Permit (1949 Geneva Convention).
  • Taiwan, Switzerland, Germany, France, Belgium, Slovenia, Monaco: an official Japanese translation of your license can be used instead of an IDP.
  • Licenses issued in mainland China cannot be used to drive in Japan.

FAQ

Can I pick up the car at Naha Airport?
Not inside the airport (local rule). Take our free shuttle from bus stop 11-B, or choose delivery to your hotel.
Can you deliver the car to my hotel?
Yes, in Naha City and Tomigusuku City. ¥1,000 each way.
We are a group of 9 or more. What should we do?
Our largest cars seat 8. Please book two cars, for example a minivan and a compact.
What do I need to drive in Japan?
Passport, your home-country license, and an International Driving Permit. Taiwan: official Japanese translation instead of an IDP.
What are your hours?
9:00-19:00, open every day.
