How to Use Our Service

 

✅

Step 1: Reservation

Please complete your reservation on the booking website.

  1. Select your desired dates and vehicle type.
  2. Select your options.
  3. Pick-up information: Enter the pick-up method, time, and location.
  4. Return information: Enter the return method, time, and location.
  5. Rental agreement: Agree to the Terms of Use (rental terms and conditions).
  6. Payment: Pay in advance on the website.

 

✅

Step 3: Pick-up (Departure)

We will hand over the vehicle using the method you selected when making your reservation.

 

  • Free shuttle (outbound)

Please use our scheduled shuttle bus from the airport to our office.

Time: Departs Naha Airport at the top of every hour (:00) (operating hours 9:00-19:00)

Location: Naha Airport Rental Car Shuttle Bus Stop [11-B]

⚠️The bus departs on schedule, so please arrive with plenty of time to spare.

 

  • Delivery option

We will deliver the vehicle to the time and location you specified in advance.

Time: 9:00-19:00

Location: Limited to Naha City and Tomigusuku City

⚠️Naha Airport is not available due to local ordinance. Please consider Akamine Station, one stop from the airport station.

 

  • Direct visit (no shuttle)

Please come directly to the HANDYMAN Second Parking Lot.

Time: 9:00-19:00

Location: HANDYMAN Second Parking Lot (details here)

⚠️If you let us know your estimated arrival time via LINE in advance, the procedure will go more smoothly.

 

 

✅

Step 4: Check-in

  1. Present driver's licenses: Please present the driver's licenses of everyone who will drive, on site.
  2. Important information: We will explain the rental rules and what to do in case of trouble.

 

 

✅

Step 5: Return

Please return the vehicle using the method you selected when making your reservation.

 

  • Free shuttle (return trip)

Please use our scheduled shuttle bus from our office to the airport.

After filling up the fuel tank, please arrive at the Second Parking Lot at least 10 minutes before departure.

Time: Departs our office at 30 minutes past every hour (operating hours 9:30-18:30)

Location: HANDYMAN Second Parking Lot (details here)

⚠️The bus departs on schedule, so please arrive with plenty of time to spare.

 

  • Delivery (collection) option

We will come to collect the vehicle at the time and location you specified in advance.

After filling up the fuel tank, please notify us via LINE when you arrive at the return location.

Time: 9:00-19:00

Location: Limited to Naha City and Tomigusuku City

⚠️Naha Airport is not available due to local ordinance. Please consider Akamine Station, one stop from the airport station.

 

  • Return only (no shuttle)

After filling up the fuel tank, please return the vehicle directly to the HANDYMAN Second Parking Lot.

Time: 9:00-19:00

Location: HANDYMAN Second Parking Lot (details here)

⚠️If you let us know your estimated arrival time via LINE in advance, the procedure will go more smoothly.
