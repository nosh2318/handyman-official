How to Use

HANDYMAN RENTCAR Sapporo Store (Delivery only)
✅

STEP 1: Booking

First, please complete your reservation.

  1. Select your travel dates and vehicle class.
  2. Select any options you need.
  3. Delivery details: Enter the time and location.
  4. Collection details: Enter the time and location.
  5. Rental agreement: Please agree to our rental terms and conditions.
  6. Payment: Pay in advance on our website.

 

✅

STEP 2: Add Our Official LINE Account

On the day of your rental, we will contact you and answer your questions mainly through LINE.
Please add our official LINE account as a friend.

  1. How to register: Add us as a friend via the URL or ID search below.

    LINE Official 👉 https://lin.ee/g6iDNYz
    LINE ID 👉 @730kyhwl

  2. Name and booking number: We will match your booking using the name and booking number from your reservation.

 

⚠️ Please complete these steps as soon as possible after your booking is confirmed.

 

✅

STEP 3: Delivery (Departure)

We will deliver your vehicle to the time and place you specified in advance.

We will send you the exact meeting point on LINE shortly before or after we arrive.

Time: 9:00-19:00

Area: Within Sapporo City and within approx. 20 km of Sapporo Station

 

To change your time or location, please use My Page, which is issued after you submit the LINE request form.

 

✅

STEP 4: Check-in (via My Page)

  1. Driver's license: All drivers must present their driver's license.
  2. Signing the rental terms and conditions
  3. Checking the vehicle for existing damage

My Page is issued in order after you submit the LINE request form.

⚠️

If you hold a driver's license issued outside Japan

Every driver must bring all of the following:

  • Passport
  • Your home country driver's license
  • An International Driving Permit (for countries party to the 1949 Geneva Convention), or an official Japanese translation of your home license (for Taiwan, Germany, France, Belgium, Switzerland, Monaco and Slovenia)

If any of these is missing, we are not permitted by law to hand over the vehicle. Please check carefully before your trip.

 

✅

STEP 5: Return (Collection)

We will come to collect the vehicle at the time and place you specified in advance.

Please refuel the tank to full, then message us on LINE once you arrive at the return location.

Time: 9:00-19:00

Area: Within Sapporo City and within approx. 20 km of Sapporo Station

 

To change your time or location, please use My Page, which is issued after you submit the LINE request form.
